02 / OVERVIEW

A modular integration concept.

The structural subsystem of the BIO-LUX platform is based on a COTS 6U CubeSat structure manufactured by 2NDSpace (model VERSE-06).

Adopting a COTS structure significantly reduces development risk while guaranteeing compliance with the CubeSat Design Specification (CDS). The layout strictly controls the Center of Mass (CoM) drift and optimizes mechanical compatibility through standard PC104 PCB stack layouts.

06 / DESIGN DECISIONS

Design decisions

COTS Structure Validation

A trade-off comprising 13 suppliers concluded the 2NDSpace VERSE-06 optimizes mass (955 g) without sacrificing integration modularity, thus avoiding the long-lead verification risks of a custom-machined chassis.

Propulsion Center of Mass Alignment

The propulsion module occupies less than 1U and is explicitly centered on the -X face. This negates severe moment arms between the thrust vector and the S/C Center of Mass, preventing uncontrollable disturbance torques.

Distributed 3U Payload Accommodation

The 3U payload budget is deliberately split. 1U is allocated to the nadir face (-X) for unobstructed camera tracking, while the remaining 2U are nested internally (+Z/+X faces) allowing deep-space thermal isolation for biological and tech payloads.

Magnetic Cleanliness Routing

To prevent internal electromagnetic interference, the AOCS Magnetometer (MTM) is positioned at the extreme end of the chassis, maximizing its physical separation from the Reaction Wheels and Magnetorquers.
