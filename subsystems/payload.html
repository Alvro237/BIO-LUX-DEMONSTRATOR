06 / DESIGN DECISIONS

Design decisions

Gecko Imager as Sizing Baseline

While the AAC Clyde IM200 is used for actual light pollution observations, the heavier and power-hungry Dragonfly Gecko acts as the sizing baseline to guarantee maximum structural and power capacity.

Off-Nadir Pointing Strategy

To increase the swath width up to 350 km and achieve revisit time requirements without exceeding a 100m GSD, a maximum off-nadir angle of 10° was implemented.

Thermal Isolation for Bio-PL

The biological module is structurally isolated and supported by a dedicated 4W continuous heater configuration to maintain strict 15 ± 10 °C boundaries regardless of external LEO cold phases.
