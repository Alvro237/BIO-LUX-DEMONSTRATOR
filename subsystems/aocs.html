04 / AOCS

Attitude & Orbit Control

Precision pointing and stabilization for Earth observation and orbital maneuvers.

AOCS Lead

Sergio Hernández

02 / OVERVIEW

AOCS provides 3-axis stabilization using a reaction wheel-based fine pointing system..

Fine attitude determination is performed with a Star Tracker aided by an IMU into an Extended Kalman Filter, while coarse and safe-mode functions rely on sun sensors and the magnetometer..

The AOCS guarantees an Absolute Pointing Error (APE) of ≤ 0.28° and minimizes image smear during high-resolution optical payload operations..

Absolute Pointing Error ≤ 0.28°
Max Image Smear (RPE) ≤ 0.004°/s
Detumbling Time < 3 orbits

05 / COMPONENTS

Components

01

FINE SENSOR

Star Tracker (TY-Space PST3S-K4)

High-precision star tracker providing absolute attitude knowledge for the fine-pointing loop.

02

HIGH-RATE SENSOR

IMU (ADIS16495)

Low-noise gyroscope and accelerometer propagating the attitude estimate at 4250 Hz between star-tracker updates.

03

FINE ACTUATOR

Reaction Wheels (NanoAvionics 4RW0)

Four reaction wheels arranged in a tetrahedron, providing 3-axis redundancy and precise pointing authority.

04

COARSE ACTUATOR

Magnetorquers (CubeSpace CR0004)

Three magnetorquer rods executing B-dot detumbling and continuous reaction-wheel desaturation.

05

COARSE SENSORS

Sun Sensors & Magnetometer

Six TensorTech sun sensors and an AAC Clyde fluxgate magnetometer supporting safe-mode sun-pointing and detumbling.

06

COMPUTE

Dedicated ADCS Processor

Dedicated ARM Cortex-M7 board (SAMV71Q21RT) running FreeRTOS, isolating the EKF and control loops from the primary CD&H workload.

06 / DESIGN DECISIONS

Design decisions

Tetrahedral Reaction-Wheel Configuration

Four wheels in a tetrahedral layout retain full 3-axis control even after a single wheel failure, satisfying the requirement that no single AOCS fault shall prevent safe-mode entry or recovery.

Dedicated ADCS Processor

By off-loading sensor fusion and control loops to a dedicated SAMV71Q21RT microcontroller, the EKF runs in a deterministic environment without resource contention from the primary flight computer.

B-Dot Detumbling Isolation

Detumbling relies exclusively on the magnetometer and magnetorquers using a B-dot law. The reaction wheels and star tracker remain off, conserving energy and avoiding wheel saturation at high rotation rates.
