05 / EPS · THERMAL

Power & Thermal Control

The energetic heart of BIO-LUX and the shield against LEO's extreme thermal environment.

EPS & Thermal Lead

Álvaro Casillas

02 / OVERVIEW

EPS guarantees power generation, storage, and distribution, while the Thermal subsystem maintains all components within safe limits.

Power generation relies on deployed and body-mounted triple-junction solar panels. The Power Conditioning and Distribution Unit (PCDU) uses MPPT architecture to maximize efficiency across varying sun angles.

Thermal control is mostly passive, using dedicated radiators, thermal straps for high-dissipation units (OBC, Bio-PL), and variable emittance materials (VEM). A 4W active heater protects the batteries during deep eclipses.

05 / COMPONENTS

Components

01

GENERATION

Deployable Solar Arrays (2 × 6U)

Triple-junction GaAs deployable arrays (30% BOL efficiency) plus body-mounted panels, raising peak generation to 58 W.

02

STORAGE

Li-ion Battery (Ibeos 45-WH)

4S1P Li-ion pack from Ibeos, 45 Wh capacity, 16.8 V nominal. Sized to survive the longest eclipse, the post-insertion stand-by and a full detumbling cycle with margin.

03

CONDITIONING

PCDU (Ibeos E14-150)

150 W PCDU with MPPT trackers, LCL protection, BCR/BDR battery regulation and full FDIR integration on every regulated bus.

04

THERMAL · ACTIVE

Battery Heater (4 W)

Single active 4 W heater on the unregulated bus, protecting the battery from fatal undervoltage during deep eclipses.

05

THERMAL · CONDUCTIVE

Conductive Straps & Radiators

Copper conductive straps (1 W/K) and deep-space facing radiators routing the heat dissipated by the OBC and the biological payload.

06

THERMAL · PASSIVE

VO₂ VEM Coating

Vanadium-Dioxide Variable-Emittance-Material coating acting as a secondary passive thermal demonstrator on the radiator panel.

06 / DESIGN DECISIONS

Design decisions

MPPT over DET Architecture

Maximum Power Point Tracking is preferred over Direct Energy Transfer: with deployable arrays at varying sun angles, MPPT continuously optimises the operating point and extracts a substantially larger fraction of the available power.

Deployable Arrays for Power Margin

Non-deployable panels would simplify mechanics but cap the available power. The 2 × 6U deployable configuration raises peak power to 58 W, leaving margin to run propulsion (45 W) and several payloads concurrently.

Passive-First Thermal Management

Thermal control is strictly passive whenever possible — optical coatings, radiators, conductive straps — to conserve power. The only active element is the battery heater, essential to avoid fatal undervoltage during long eclipses.
